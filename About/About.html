Our Services

VeriSafe helps older adults, families, and small businesses protect themselves online.

Who We Are

Verisafe is an organization of tech students from the University of South Carolina - Columbia dedicated to lending a helping hand to those who seek assistance or clarity about the ever-growing technical world.

Our Mission

To bridge language and generational gaps in digital safety by empowering families, seniors, and local businesses with practical, culture-conscious cybersecurity training.

Why We Exist

We are a team of cybersecurity students dedicated to improving online safety. We believe that digital security shouldn't be limited by age, technical background, or language barriers. By delivering practical training in the languages our communities speak, we help individuals navigate the internet with confidence.

Our Values

  • Accessible and actionable security guidance.
  • Clear and accurate information.
  • Guidance that is easy to understand and implement.
